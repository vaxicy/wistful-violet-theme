Wistful Violet Theme dresses the Chrome window in a nostalgic evening palette. The frame and tab strip sit in a deep violet, the toolbar and bookmark bar lift one shade lighter, and the new tab page opens on a soft lavender canvas with a dusky mauve wordmark. A single violet accent, taken from the theme's own button tint, runs through the icon and the store artwork. Every surface is one flat solid colour, and the text tones are tuned so tab titles, toolbar icons, bookmark labels and address bar text stay comfortable to read against the dark frame.
